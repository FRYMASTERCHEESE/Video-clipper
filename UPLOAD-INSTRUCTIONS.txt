WHAT ORIGINALITY GUARD AUTOMATES
- Detects externally sourced / Commons footage
- Builds a different educational storyline for every generated Short
- Generates 5 distinct educational commentary/caption cards
- Burns those commentary cards visibly into the final 9:16 video
- Keeps the existing SRT captions
- Preserves source attribution
- Checks repeated source combinations during the session
- Uses multi-source count from Animal AI montages
- If only one external source is used, forces the upload to Private for manual review
- If attribution cannot be confirmed, forces Private
- If visible-commentary rendering fails, forces Private
- Adds an Originality Guard status panel to the website
- Works alongside Premium Auto unique titles, brighter thumbnails and category pages
