ClipFree Turbo v18 — MOBILE ONLY

This update fixes the long "finding a moving unused source" wait and also makes
mobile encoding + YouTube transfer smaller/faster.

UPLOAD/REPLACE TWO FILES IN FRYMASTERCHEESE/Video-clipper:
1. compliance-demo-v11.js
2. video-engine-v7.js

What v18 changes:

SOURCE FINDING
- Removes the expensive v17 remote-seeking checks that could stall for many minutes.
- Searches actual VIDEO MIME files only.
- Prefers <=12 MB source videos first.
- Uses one <=20 MB fallback search only if needed.
- Keeps licence filtering.
- Final Motion Guard still blocks repeated-still/picture-only Shorts before upload.
- Source retry count reduced from 6 to 3 to avoid long retry loops.

MOBILE VIDEO ENCODING
- Phones use 720x1280 vertical HD.
- x264 ultrafast preset.
- ~1.5 Mbps max video bitrate.
- 96 kbps AAC narration.
- Smaller MP4 = less encoding work + less data to upload.

IMPORTANT
- This does not make your phone's internet connection physically faster.
- It removes avoidable ClipFree waiting and makes the upload file smaller.
- Keep the browser tab open during a multi-Short run.

After uploading both files:
1. Commit to main.
2. Wait 1–2 minutes.
3. Fully close old coreyvibe.org tabs.
4. Reopen ClipFree.
5. Connect YouTube.
6. Try 8 Shorts again.
