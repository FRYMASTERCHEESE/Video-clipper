ClipFree 20 Shorts v14 — MOBILE ONLY

This keeps Motion Guard v13 and replaces the old 8+8+4 batch behavior with
a safer sequential 20-Short runner.

New behavior:
- Runs 1 Short at a time until all 20 are done.
- Every Short must receive a REAL YouTube video ID before the next starts.
- Motion Guard v13 still blocks still-picture Shorts.
- If any Short fails, ClipFree stops at that exact Short and shows the real error.
- Successful uploads already completed are kept.
- It never claims 20/20 unless all 20 real video IDs are verified.

Replace this ONE repo file:
  compliance-demo-v11.js

Mobile steps:
1. Download the ZIP or JS file.
2. In FRYMASTERCHEESE/Video-clipper, replace compliance-demo-v11.js.
3. Commit to main.
4. Wait a few minutes.
5. Fully close old coreyvibe.org tabs and reopen the site.
6. Connect YouTube.
7. Select 20, tick rights, and press Create + SEO + Upload 20 Shorts.
8. Keep the browser tab open while the 20 sequential uploads run.
