Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026.

ClipFree AI is designed as a static browser application. Video clipping, transcription and most media processing are performed on your device in your browser. ClipFree AI does not operate its own account database or upload server in this build.

Google and YouTube data

When you choose to connect YouTube, Google handles sign-in and provides a temporary OAuth access token. The token is used to request YouTube channel data, analytics and actions that you explicitly initiate or enable in the app. The access token is kept in page memory and is not written to the GitHub repository.

The Google OAuth Client ID and optional YouTube API key that you enter may be stored in your browser's localStorage so the setup persists on that device. You can clear them from the Setup section.

Videos and media

Local video files are processed in your browser. If you use reusable-media discovery, your browser may request files and metadata from Wikimedia Commons. If you upload to YouTube, the finished media and metadata are sent directly from your browser to Google's YouTube APIs.

Donation links

The optional charity button only opens the external charity or fundraiser URL configured by the site owner on that browser. ClipFree AI does not process, collect or hold donation payments.

Third-party services

The app can load libraries or data from Google, YouTube, Wikimedia Commons, jsDelivr, unpkg, Hugging Face and related content-delivery services. Those services may receive normal web request information such as IP address and browser headers under their own privacy policies.

Control

You can disconnect YouTube, clear saved settings, close the page, or clear browser site data at any time.
