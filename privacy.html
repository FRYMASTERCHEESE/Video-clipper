Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026.

ClipFree AI is a browser-based media workflow tool that uses YouTube API Services when you choose to connect a YouTube channel. Video clipping, transcription and most media processing are performed on your device in your browser. The current production build is hosted as a static website and does not operate its own account database or upload server.

Google user data ClipFree AI accesses

ClipFree AI uses YouTube API Services. When you choose to connect your YouTube account, Google handles sign-in and provides ClipFree AI with an OAuth access token for the permissions you approve. ClipFree AI does not ask for, receive or store your Google Account password.

Depending on the features you use, ClipFree AI may access your connected YouTube channel identity and channel metadata, uploaded-video metadata, playlists and playlist items, captions and thumbnail-related data needed for creator tools, and YouTube Analytics reports such as views, watch time, subscriber movement and top-performing content.

The current production build requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl for visible YouTube creator, upload, caption, playlist and channel-management features, and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly for the visible analytics dashboard.

How ClipFree AI uses Google user data

ClipFree AI uses Google user data only to provide user-facing features that you request or enable. These uses include showing your connected YouTube channel, recent videos, playlists and analytics; generating channel and video optimization suggestions; uploading videos, captions and thumbnails that you instruct ClipFree AI to publish; and adding uploaded videos to playlists when you select or enable that option.

ClipFree AI does not use Google user data for advertising, ad targeting, credit decisions, lending, surveillance, data brokerage, information resale or unrelated profiling. ClipFree AI does not use Google user data to create, train, develop or improve generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

Who we share, transfer or disclose Google user data to

ClipFree AI does not sell, rent or disclose Google user data to advertisers, data brokers, information resellers or unrelated third parties.

Google user data is shared, transferred or disclosed only in these limited circumstances:

  • Google and YouTube API Services: your browser exchanges authorized Google user data directly with Google/YouTube only as necessary to perform the user-facing YouTube features you request, such as loading your channel or analytics, uploading content, managing captions, thumbnails or playlists, or revoking access.
  • No separate ClipFree AI backend: the current production build does not operate a server that receives or stores your OAuth access token, YouTube Analytics reports or other Google user data. ClipFree AI operators do not receive routine human access to this data.
  • Legal or security requirements: Google user data may be disclosed only if required by applicable law, regulation or legal process, or when reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, abuse or a security threat.

ClipFree AI uses third-party hosting and browser libraries to deliver the website, including services such as GitHub Pages, jsDelivr and unpkg, and it can obtain reusable-media resources from services such as Wikimedia Commons. ClipFree AI does not intentionally send Google user data or OAuth access tokens to these services. They may receive ordinary web-request information such as an IP address and browser headers when your browser loads their resources, under their own privacy policies.

Data protection mechanisms for sensitive Google user data

ClipFree AI uses the following technical and organizational safeguards to protect Google user data and OAuth credentials:

  • Encryption in transit: the website and communications with Google and YouTube API Services use HTTPS/TLS-secured connections.
  • OAuth 2.0: Google handles authentication and consent. ClipFree AI never asks for your Google Account password.
  • Access tokens stay in browser memory: OAuth access tokens are held in page memory for the active browser session and are not written to localStorage or stored in a ClipFree AI server database.
  • No client secret in the website: the public browser application does not embed or store a Google OAuth client secret.
  • Minimum necessary permissions: access is limited to the OAuth scopes and user-facing functions described in this policy.
  • No advertising disclosure: ClipFree AI does not intentionally send OAuth access tokens or Google user data to advertising networks, data brokers or information resellers.
  • Revocation and clearing: you can disconnect YouTube at any time. When a token is available, ClipFree AI requests token revocation through Google's authorization library and clears Google/YouTube data held in page state. You can also revoke ClipFree AI directly from your Google Account permissions.

Because the current production build does not maintain a server-side Google user-data store, there is no ClipFree AI server database containing OAuth access tokens or YouTube Analytics data. ClipFree AI nevertheless takes reasonable steps to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or destruction of data handled by the application.

Browser storage

The Google OAuth Client ID, optional public YouTube API key and selected application settings may be stored in this browser's localStorage so setup persists on this device. These values are not OAuth access tokens. You can remove them with the Clear saved settings control or by clearing this site's browser data.

Retention, revocation and deletion

ClipFree AI retains Google user data only for as long as needed to provide the features you request during the active browser session. The current static build does not persist channel analytics or OAuth access tokens on a ClipFree AI server.

You can disconnect YouTube at any time using the Disconnect control. ClipFree AI clears the YouTube data held in page state and, when a token is available, requests revocation through Google's authorization library. You can also revoke ClipFree AI from your Google Account permissions.

Deleting locally stored ClipFree AI settings does not delete videos or other content stored by YouTube. To delete content stored by YouTube, use YouTube or another authorized client that supports that action.

Limited Use disclosure

ClipFree AI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

ClipFree AI does not transfer Google user data to advertising platforms, data brokers or information resellers; does not use Google user data for serving advertisements; does not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes; and does not use it to create, train or improve generalized or non-personalized AI or machine-learning models.

Videos and media

Local video files are processed in your browser. If you use reusable-media discovery, your browser may request files and metadata from Wikimedia Commons. If you upload to YouTube, the finished media and metadata are sent directly from your browser to Google's YouTube APIs.

Your choices and consent

ClipFree AI identifies the connected YouTube channel and shows the visibility setting used for uploads. Upload and other write actions are performed only after you initiate or enable the workflow and confirm that you have the rights to upload the content and that it complies with YouTube's applicable policies.

Google and YouTube policies

Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Donation links

The optional charity button only opens the external charity or fundraiser URL configured by the site owner on that browser. ClipFree AI does not process, collect or hold donation payments.

Contact and data requests

For privacy or data-handling questions about ClipFree AI, contact coreyvibe.music@gmail.com. This current build is a static self-hosted tool, so you control the browser data on your device. Use Disconnect and Clear saved settings to revoke access and remove local setup data. If ClipFree AI later adds a hosted account or backend, this policy will be updated with the applicable hosted-data retention, security and deletion practices before that service is offered.
