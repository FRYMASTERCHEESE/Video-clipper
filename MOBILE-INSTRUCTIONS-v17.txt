ClipFree Fast Real Videos v17 — MOBILE ONLY

This fixes the slow "finding a moving unused source" step.

Why v16 was slow:
It could fully download many candidate videos one-by-one before deciding whether
they contained real movement.

v17:
- searches smaller real video files first
- checks up to 3 candidates at the same time
- streams only the video sections needed for motion checking
- does NOT fully download every rejected candidate
- uses short timeouts so one bad/slow source cannot hold the whole batch up
- still rejects picture-only / repeated-frame videos
- still keeps v15 sequential 2–20 uploads and Motion Guard safety

Replace ONE file in GitHub:
  compliance-demo-v11.js

Then:
1. Commit to main.
2. Wait 1–2 minutes.
3. Fully close old coreyvibe.org tabs.
4. Reopen ClipFree.
5. Connect YouTube.
6. Try 8 Shorts again.
7. Keep the tab open while it works.

Actual rendering and internet upload time still depend on your phone, source file,
connection speed and YouTube. The source-finding stage should no longer spend
many minutes downloading rejected candidates.
